## Rights and engineering boundaries

- **Apache-2.0:** permissive including commercial reuse; preserve notices/license and comply with its patent terms.
- **GPL-3.0-only:** open source including commercial use subject to copyleft and applicable corresponding-source obligations when conveying covered derivative programs. The COSMOS Music repository was restored to its historical GPL family.
- **MIT:** permissive, retains attribution requirements and upstream authors' copyright notices.
- **Special exceptions:** named documentation can carry CC BY 4.0 where indicated; third-party runtimes, SDKs, biometric or medical data, private footage, source recordings, model weights and external artwork retain independent terms.
- **Project ownership:** released code remains copyrighted by its legitimate authors; existing open-source grants cannot be retroactively taken back from people who received that version under the license. Future new original work can be sold or separately licensed where all necessary rights are controlled.
- **Historical license chronology:** inspect each project's `LICENSE_HISTORY.md` / `LICENSE-HISTORY.md` where present. Research publication citation and software licensing are different rights.

This document records repository-root licensing changes merged on 2026-09-27. It does not make the four private workspaces public; reviewing or releasing private/sensitive records requires a separate decision and redaction audit.

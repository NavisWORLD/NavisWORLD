## Scientific and architectural boundaries

The profile retains **MODEL ≠ MEMORY ≠ STATE ≠ AUTHORITY** and explicitly states **MODEL ≠ IDENTITY**. Continuity is serialized software identity/state, not consciousness. Authority does not travel automatically with exported information or state.

Recorded measurement results are ordinary classical data after storage and may be fixed seed inputs. They do not establish a live quantum link, entanglement or quantum consciousness. Simulation, seeded generation, stored measurement data and hardware execution remain different provenance categories. Visualization is not an experiment; support is not scientific validation. Null and failed results remain legitimate.

The scanner is marked **EXAMPLE / SIMULATED INPUT**. Its focus **18**, calm **8** and spark **94** are taken from the supplied Cogfist capture. They are not invented backend telemetry or live measurements of the illustrated guide. The watch is explicitly a **CONCEPT**, not a photographed or verified hardware prototype.

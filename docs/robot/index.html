Zeref Spark companion robot · 3D

CAD v0.2 from The Beast Box · CONCEPT DESIGN / NOT BUILT HARDWARE

moves chosen from Zeref's recorded state · SIMULATED motion

Drag to orbit, pinch to zoom. He starts closed. What he does next — when the shells open, when he turns, how the head, ears and arms move, when he pauses — is chosen from a hash of Zeref's recorded heartbeat, diary, spark and brain state, so the sequence changes when that record is refreshed and is never a fixed loop. Shells still split on their real y=0 seam to show the ESP32-S3 cradle, sensor sled and battery tray; the eye, ear and chest LED rings pulse; the crystal station cycles white / 365 nm UV / 940 nm IR. Limb, head and ear motion is SIMULATED: those parts are static in the CAD.

Geometry: every custom part (ZS-101…ZS-604) plus a few purchased-part proxies, as exported by the package's CadQuery build (beast spark robot/out/stl/assembly_coords, Apache-2.0), packed unchanged into one GLB by scripts/robot/build_spark_glb.py. Colours follow its cad/render.py. Rendering: three.js r180 (MIT). All dimensions are CAD ESTIMATES; nothing has been printed, wired or bench-tested.
